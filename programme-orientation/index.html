3 Months From today to your final readout on 16 July.
7 Modules Clustered in 1 to 3 days each, with practice time in between.

Day 1 | Spoken commitments

One sentence each, said out loud.

Use this sentence shape

"I will do something specific by a date, because an honest reason."

Specific. "I will complete Module 1 with a defendable triage by 21 April, because trust in our extracts is overdue."
Dated. "I will produce a branch performance dashboard by week six, because my team needs it next quarter."
Honest. "I will present a real AI recommendation to my function head by certificate week, because I want to lead this conversation."

No "I'll try". Specific. Dated. Honest. The cohort needs to hear your promise. You need to hear yourself make it.

Day 1 | Bridge into Module 1

Your warm-up tonight before Python tomorrow.

Twenty minutes of thinking, no code, no installs. Bring the output to tomorrow morning. The first lab opens with exactly this material.

Tonight (about 20 min)

  • Find one extract. Pull up a real banking report, dashboard, or extract you receive regularly. Note how many columns it has, roughly how many rows, and the one number on it you trust the least.
  • Write one question. On a single line, write the question you would want a script to answer about that extract. Honest. Small. Concrete. For example: "How many transactions in this extract are over 50,000 AED?" or "Which customers appear more than once and why?"
  • Click into Module 1 once. Open the Module 1 tile in the studio at least once tonight so it is loaded on your machine before the morning.

What happens tomorrow morning

  • Same time, same room. Module 1 starts at the top of the hour.
  • Five-minute reconnect with the cohort.
  • You read your warm-up question aloud in the first 15 minutes; we use it to ground the first lab.
  • Hands on a notebook within the first hour. You will not be writing Python from a blank page; you will be answering the question you already wrote down.

Take twenty quiet minutes tonight to do this. Post in the cohort feed if anything blocks you and I will see it before the morning.
